Chat witha book

A conversation runs out of room long before it runs out of things to say. Two endpoints fix that: context/trim counts what you are carrying, context/compact folds the old part into a summary. Take a whole book off the shelf below — or paste your own document, or fetch a URL — and watch the fold happen. Anything that overflows the model window outright is folded in sections first: a summary is only useful if it fits. A fold is not a shredder, though: every folded section is still here. Dive into one to ask a question against its full original text, refold it around what you actually care about, or watch the assistant reopen a section by itself when your question needs a detail the digest dropped.
