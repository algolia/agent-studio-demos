Identifier filtering, before and after

A bank customer types an account number into chat. Without a filter, it reaches the model and the chat log. With one, a mask takes its place.

Before
The raw number reaches the model, the cache and the chat log.
After, redact
A placeholder such as [IBAN] goes on in its place.
After, block
The message is refused before the raw number is stored or sent.
