    Methodology — read this before quoting any number

    Order-of-magnitude planning aids, not quotes. Three rules:

    1. Every constant is on this page; readers audit numbers instead of trusting them.
    2. Every figure is a ±50% band; deployments land inside it when the assumptions hold.
    3. A model without a defensible price shows no cost. “No curated price” rows count tokens; they refuse to invent dollars.

    Price scopes. Platform rows use the exact per-1M table your bill comes from — estimate and invoice cannot drift. List rows are published, dated prices. Placeholders say so.

    How this maps to packaging. At GA, tokens leave the invoice: plans carry a platform fee plus an interaction envelope — one interaction per turn; cache hits, guardrail blocks and bots excluded; alerts at 80% and 100%. The managed model is included, never priced in tokens — these bands are the BYOK stand-in. Not priced: embeddings, indexing, storage.

    Arithmetic mirrors the platform's estimation service; every price names its source; corrections go to data.js.
